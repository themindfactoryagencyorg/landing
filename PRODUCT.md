# TheMindFactory landing

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners and operations teams evaluating a partner to build software around their existing work. The first visit should explain the offer, show relevant work and make contacting the team easy.

## Product Purpose

Present custom applications, integrations and automation. Give the AI agent a prominent place as a main product, while making the wider custom development offer equally clear. Use the supplied David presentation as the source for the three client examples.

## Brand Personality

Human, practical, inventive. Preserve the existing mint identity, B1 logo, local fonts, neural scene and flowing light.

## Anti-references

Abstract architecture before a clear business benefit; generic AI promises; invented statistics; a website centered on two sales contacts instead of the full team.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the visitor's work and the problem they recognise.
- Show existing solutions before explaining internal product names.
- Present illustrative agent interactions explicitly as examples.
- Explain what happens after the first conversation.
- Preserve all seven team profiles and both languages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep the existing keyboard, language, reduced motion and no-JavaScript fallbacks. Use native disclosures for optional detail and readable contrast in both dark and light sections.
